# Sources and methodological attribution

## 1. Published foundations

- Altavilla, C., Brugnolini, L., Gürkaynak, R. S., Motto, R. and Ragusa, G. (2019). [Measuring euro area monetary policy](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmoneco.2019.08.016). *Journal of Monetary Economics*, 108, 162–179. The published article text was consulted during the feasibility review. It provides the EA-MPD foundation; a full methods review remains part of the design stage.
- Jarociński, M. and Karadi, P. (2020). [Deconstructing Monetary Policy Surprises: The Role of Information Shocks](https://doi.org/10.1257/mac.20180090). *American Economic Journal: Macroeconomics*, 12(2), 1–43. Publication details and the publisher's abstract were verified; the update repository documentation was consulted. A full-text methods review and replication check remain outstanding.
- Jordà, Ò. (2005). [Estimation and Inference of Impulse Responses by Local Projections](https://doi.org/10.1257/0002828053828518). *American Economic Review*, 95(1), 161–182. I consulted the [publisher's metadata and abstract](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/0002828053828518) on 30 September 2026 to attribute the proposed local-projection method. I have not reviewed the full text for this project. Specification, inference and implementation remain part of the design stage; no local-projection estimates have been produced.

These citations identify established research. The author-maintained data update is a subsequent vintage and must not be described as the original journal replication dataset.

## 2. Data sources

- [Eurostat industrial production](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sts_inpr_m/default/table?lang=en), `sts_inpr_m`: Germany, production volume, monthly, seasonally and calendar adjusted, 2021=100, `MIG_DCOG` and `MIG_NDCOG`.
- [Eurostat HICP](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/en/web/products-datasets/-/PRC_HICP_MIDX), `prc_hicp_midx`: Germany, monthly, all-items `CP00`, 2015=100.
- [Jarociński's ECB shock update](https://github.com/marekjarocinski/jkshocks_update_ecb): unchanged monthly and event CSV files, CC BY 4.0. The repository's README was consulted for construction, units and licensing. Its software is not copied into this project.
- [ECB EA-MPD workbook](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/pub/pdf/annex/Dataset_EA-MPD.xlsx): accessed for the preliminary event-coverage check; not included in the public snapshot.

Access date for the feasibility snapshot: 15 September 2026. Exact download URLs and checksums are in the [manifest](../data/raw/2026-09-15/manifest.json). See [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for reuse conditions. No paper prose, table or figure is reproduced in this project.
