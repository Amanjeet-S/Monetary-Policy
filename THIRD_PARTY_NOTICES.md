# Third-party notices

## 1. Numerical sources

US consumption quantities, prices and income are estimates of the Bureau of Economic Analysis; effective funds-rate observations originate in Federal Reserve H.15. They are distributed through FRED. The [snapshot manifest](data/raw/2026-10-11-us-purchases/manifest.json) identifies exact series, original bytes and official source links. Transformations are documented in the [data guide](data/README.md).

The unchanged original policy workbook is by Christina D. Romer and David H. Romer, accompanying their 2004 American Economic Review article, [DOI 10.1257/0002828042002651](https://doi.org/10.1257/0002828042002651), obtained from their university data page. Original source authorship and applicable rights remain intact. The repository licence does not relicense third-party data. Published article texts are excluded.
