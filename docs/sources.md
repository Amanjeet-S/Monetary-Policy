# Sources and consultation

## 1. Published identification and methods

The consultation record below distinguishes published text from accessible predecessors. Unless stated otherwise, the design-stage consultation date is 10 October 2026. These are established methods; neither their implementation nor the projection decomposition constitutes a new theoretical contribution.

These studies establish durability heterogeneity. They do not supply an estimate in the same production categories, units and design as the proposed matched German policy/information comparison. The retained application assesses this matched contrast; the focused consultation does not prove that it has never been studied. The household benchmark and measurement bridge added on 11 October 2026 interpret the existing estimates without changing the locked empirical specification.

## 3. Explicitly unpublished supplements

Flaccadoro, M. and Ropele, T. (2026), [Asymmetric monetary policy transmission across euro-area manufacturing industries](https://doi.org/10.32057/0.TD.2026.1535), Bank of Italy Working Paper 1535, June. I consulted the official text, §§1–3, 4.1–4.2, 5.3 and Appendix A, with relevant figure and table captions. It already combines consumer MIG production, information-adjusted ECB shocks and monthly projections; §5.3 also estimates a broad-durability differential. Its two-digit mapping is not the detailed official MIG definition. No published successor was located in the focused title search; this is a preliminary working paper and a particularly close comparison.

Jarociński, M. and Karadi, P. (2026), [Monetary policy surprises with imperfect information](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3292~76d878a0cf.en.pdf), ECB Working Paper 3292, October. I consulted the abstract, non-technical summary, introduction and §2.1, pp. 1–10. It distinguishes outlook information from reaction-function misperception in Fed communication. It supplements the current identification debate; its unpublished estimates do not establish an ECB or German result and do not replace the 2020 journal article.
